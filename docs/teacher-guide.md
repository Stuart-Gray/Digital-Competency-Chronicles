# Teacher guide (draft skeleton)

This guide grows with the game. The full English and French guide for the Dungeon 2 slice is
planned for milestone 3.

## What the game is (and is not)

- A short adventure game that lets students explore the Ministry's *Content for Developing
  Digital Competency* through play.
- **Not an evaluation.** The content is not a subject and is not evaluated. The game shows no
  marks, scores or percentages. Students collect achievements and badges, and wrong answers
  lead to a hint and another try.
- Feedback happens the Ministry's way: observation, spontaneous questions and brief discussion.
  Each puzzle comes with a discussion question for you.

## Getting started

1. Open the game: https://stuart-gray.github.io/Digital-Competency-Chronicles/
2. Students press **Play**, pick a free slot, choose a character and (optionally) type their
   first name. Up to five students can save on one device.
3. They choose their cycle: **Cycle 2** (Grades 3 and 4) or **Cycle 3** (Grades 5 and 6).
   The words, scenarios and puzzles change with the cycle.
4. The game saves by itself. **Menu → Save and go to the title screen** ends a session; the
   student picks the same slot next time to continue where they left off.

Sessions are designed for 10 to 15 minutes.

## Settings for accessibility

- **English / Français** on the title screen and in Settings.
- **Read aloud** uses the device's built-in voices. All game text is real web text, so
  Read&Write (text-to-speech, hover to read) and screen readers can read it too.
- **Text speed**, **Large text** and **High contrast**.
- **Music** on or off, and **Music volume** (quiet, medium, loud). Music starts after the first
  tap or key press and is on by default at medium volume; turn it off for silent work time.
  The setting is saved on the device, so it stays off for the next player too.
- Keyboard (arrows or WASD, Space or Enter to talk) or tap/click to move: tap a spot and the hero
  walks there; tap a person or thing to walk up and talk or look. Both work everywhere. On tablets,
  an on-screen arrow pad can be turned on in Settings.

## Privacy

No accounts, no tracking, nothing sent anywhere. Progress is saved only in the browser on the
device. The only personal information is an optional first name. **Teacher corner** (press and
hold the button on the title screen) lets you clear one save or all saves on the device.

## What is in the game so far

| Where | Element | What students do | Ask afterwards |
|---|---|---|---|
| Village, Théo's Fix-it shop | 4b Apply troubleshooting strategies | Put the help steps in order (try yourself, Help section, a classmate, the teacher), then choose the best first step when an app freezes | Why is it a good idea to try on your own before asking for help? What could we add to our class troubleshooting poster? |
| Temple of Traces, Hall of Traces (one session) | 2a Understand digital identity | Match online actions (photo, like, comment) to what they reveal; watch a "deleted" post get copied; choose a profile picture that doesn't show your face | What could someone learn about you from your last three online actions? Why might a deleted post still matter? |
| Temple of Traces, Vault of Keys (one session) | 2b Understand personal information | Sort what is personal information; decide what to do when a stranger asks for it; sort easy- and hard-to-guess passwords | What words in a message make you think "this might be a trick"? How can a password be hard to guess but easy to remember? |
| Temple of Traces, Window Gallery (one session) | 2c Understand online privacy protection | Find the clues in a photo (Cycle 3: a photo changed by an AI filter); decide which app requests to allow or ask about; notice a camera and mic still on after a call | Does changing a photo with AI make it safe to share? How can we tell if a camera or microphone is on? |
| Temple of Traces, Mask Hall | 2a, 2b, 2c review | Answer the Mask Merchant's three tricks | What is one thing you would never share online? Why? |

Each wing fits a 10 to 15 minute session and ends with a badge piece; the game saves and resumes
inside the temple. The discussion questions and look-fors for area 2 are in `content/area2.json`
(`teacher`) and on each puzzle (`teacherNote`). They will appear in the teacher corner in
milestone 3, with the class progress view.
